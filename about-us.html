Covert Science GmbH

Founded in 2017, our company is built on a foundation of optical engineering, microelectronics, power electronics, and advanced materials research.

The founder possesses more than twenty years of expertise in technology development, electronics manufacturing, and R&D leadership across multiple startups and industries.

The organization collaborates with research institutions including Fraunhofer IZM to develop advanced solutions for wearables, smart textiles, and high-performance electronic components.
