About Us

Pioneering innovation in optical engineering, microelectronics, and advanced materials since 2017

Our Story

Covert Science GmbH was established in 2017 with a clear mission: to empower manufacturers with smart, scalable, and cost-effective innovation. Our founder brings over two decades of experience in technology development, electronics manufacturing, and R&D leadership.

With a foundation in optical engineering, microelectronics, and power electronics, we are driven by cutting-edge research and development to create solutions that push the boundaries of what's possible.

Core Focus Areas

  • Wearables development and smart textiles
  • High-performance electronic components
  • Flexible electronics and bio-feedback applications
  • Next-generation optical systems
  • Power electronics and embedded systems

Strategic Partnerships

We believe in the power of collaboration. Our strategic partnerships enable us to deliver comprehensive solutions and stay at the forefront of technological innovation.

  • Fraunhofer IZM - Research collaboration
  • Photonics Cluster Berlin Brandenburg - Industry network
  • Innovation Network for Advanced Materials - Materials research
